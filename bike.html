THE MACHINE

IMR Corsa 190RR

The IMR Corsa 190RR is a lightweight pit-bike based race machine used by Matěj Kerl for MotoGymkhana and track riding.

The compact chassis and relatively small dimensions make the bike well suited to tight technical courses where rapid direction changes and precise control are important.

The bike is focused on competition rather than road use.
