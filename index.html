ABOUT KEHI

KeHi Motorsport is a Czech motorcycle racing team founded by classmates Matěj Kerl and Jan “Honza” Hingar .

The name KeHi combines the surnames Kerl and Hingar .

The team focuses on motorcycle racing, MotoGymkhana, race-bike development and building practical technical projects around motorsport.

KeHi Motorsport combines riding, engineering, fabrication and competition into one team.
